# 30-Step Implementation Map

The `dpt run` command executes and documents all 30 steps from the worked guide. Steps that are
mathematical audits rather than independent data transformations are recorded in the audit trail.

- Steps 1-9: declarations, DC/QC construction and coordinates.
- Steps 10-18: regions, validity checks, dominance, frontier, budget and procedure expansion.
- Steps 19-23: distance, neighbourhood, path, geodesic, utility and scalarisation limitation.
- Steps 24-28: threshold/weight/error sensitivity, dynamics and threshold scarcity.
- Steps 29-30: benchmark linkage and final transparent screening decision.

No software output proves truth, ethical acceptability, feasibility, fundability or discovery status.
